# Sound cues

`cue-start.wav` and `cue-stop.wav` are `maximize_008` and `minimize_008` from
[Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
converted from Ogg to WAV and otherwise unmodified.

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The app loads whatever `cue-start` / `cue-stop` audio file is in this folder (mp3, wav, aiff,
m4a or caf). Users can also pick their own files in 設定 → 音訊 → 提示音.
